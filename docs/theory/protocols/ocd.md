# Protocol — OCD, derived from the model

19 August 2026. Filed in Clinical Tools beside the Decision Aid and the other protocols;
sibling to [[Protocol — Health Anxiety]] (same certainty demand, wider jurisdiction).
**Honesty label, in the appendix spirit: made, not found.** This is a model-derived
reorganization of components that existing OCD treatments have tested separately — exposure
and response prevention (Foa lineage), the Salkovskis responsibility-appraisal model, the
intrusion-normality literature (Rachman & de Silva), and the checking-degrades-confidence work
(van den Hout & Kindt). The *sequence* is the model's contribution (gates → gate re-taught →
charter → interruption → readings → ignition → consolidation → relapse plan), and the sequence
has no outcome data of its own. Written by a counseling student; runs under supervision —
**and in this riser the supervision line is structural: ERP is collaborative, consented, and
supervised, or it is not run at all.** Companions: [[floor-notes/Floor 2 — Metacognition]],
[[floor-notes/Floor 5 — Scripts and Behavior]], the furnace (§2.3), and the Craske and Kube
pins in the ledger.

## The formulation in one line

OCD presents on floor 3 as emergencies (contamination, harm, blasphemy, error, wrongness), but
the fault is at floor 2's gate — mind-noise read as message, tried under a responsibility
charter — executes on floor 5 (rituals overt and mental, and a household recruited into
accommodation), is sometimes ignited from floor 6 (the blamed child, the scrupulous house, the
coincidence that convicted), sits in floor-8 water (purity codes, doctrine, certainty-worship),
and is worn on the mattering dial's moral edge — and the treatment follows the building, not
the content.

## The model's distinctive reading

Three claims organize everything below.

**The intrusion is normal; the appraisal is the disorder.** The founding datum (Rachman & de
Silva): intrusive thoughts with clinical OCD's exact content — harming a child, shoving the
stranger, the blasphemy mid-prayer, the stove, the germ — occur in the overwhelming majority
of ordinary minds. Every mind is a machine room; noise is what machinery sounds like. The
disorder begins at floor 2's gate, where this machine misfiles its own noise as *message*: a
thought about harm is read as a signal about danger, intention, or character — thought-action
fusion, the gate's signature failure. Panic misreads the body's noise; OCD misreads the mind's.
And the content is a values map read upside down: the machine flags what would matter most if
true, which is why the loving parent draws harm thoughts and the devout draw blasphemy — the
gate checks the most precious cargo hardest. The dignity move is built into the science: the
thoughts are not a confession; they are an inventory of what the client loves.

**Responsibility is the current that keeps the gate seized.** Salkovskis's appraisal, in the
model's terms: the charter that converts noise into emergency reads *if my thought, act, or
omission could contribute to harm, and I fail to neutralize it, the harm is mine.* The forecast
under every obsession: *if I don't act on this signal, catastrophe — and it will be my fault.*
Guilt is prepaid; the client is serving the sentence for an uncommitted crime, daily. Attached
to the charter is the certainty demand: ordinary machines close files at "almost certainly
fine"; this gate demands a *felt* 100% that no human equipment can produce — so the file never
closes, and the checking is the file being reopened for a feeling that isn't coming.

**The ritual is the perfect crime against learning — and it manufactures the doubt it
treats.** Compulsions — washing, checking, redoing, confessing, and the covert menu: counting,
praying-as-neutralizing, mental review, argument with the thought — are the dimmer at its most
elaborate: each ritual "prevents" a catastrophe that was never coming, files another save, and
blocks the one reading that would close the case. But this riser adds a mechanism no other
protocol has: **the remedy degrades the instrument.** Checking corrodes memory confidence (van
den Hout & Kindt — the tenth check leaves you *less* sure than the first), washing amplifies
contamination salience, review scrambles the record, and engaging the thought as signal raises
its gain. The machine's cure manufactures the uncertainty that demands the cure. The five
moves in costume: *avoid* (knives, driving, the child's bath, being last out), *filter* and
*reinterpret* (clean survivals credited to rituals), *provoke* (the scan that asks "am I
having the thought? do I feel certain yet?" — and summons it), *withdraw effort* (whole
domains ceded to the ritual's schedule — and a household annexed as accommodation staff).

## Phase 0 — The gates (run first, and never fully retired)

**The harm-content differential, handled with both hands.** This is the gate a student must
walk with a supervisor, every time. Ego-dystonic harm obsessions — unwanted, horrifying to the
client, fought with rituals — are OCD, carry no elevated enactment risk in the literature, and
are *treated as OCD*: misreading them as danger is iatrogenic twice over (it confiscates the
treatment and confirms the client's worst appraisal). But the assessment must actually be
done, not assumed: ego-syntonic ideation, wish-flavored content, command phenomena, intent,
plan, or a picture that doesn't fit the intrusion-plus-ritual engine route to risk protocols
and psychiatric referral instead. Postpartum intrusions get their own flag: extremely common,
terrifying, massively under-reported, and the highest-stakes misread in the drawer — a new
parent with ego-dystonic harm thoughts and checking rituals needs this protocol and
reassurance about prevalence, not a report that ends the breastfeeding.

**Risk and mood.** OCD carries heavy depression comorbidity and real suicide risk under
decades of shame — full standard screens, plus the specific question the shame hides: "some
people with these thoughts start to believe they'd be safer gone; has that visited you?"

**Routing.** Realistic-content, topic-hopping worry without rituals → [[Protocol — Worry
(GAD)]]. Disease fear fed by checking → [[Protocol — Health Anxiety]] (same machinery, single
jurisdiction — the protocols compose). Insight lost entirely, or the picture smells of
psychosis (the reversed dial) → divert per the Decision Aid; note OCD's insight runs a
spectrum and dips under load, so the differential is pattern and history, not one bad hour.
Hoarding, tics/PANDAS flags in minors, and body-focused repetitive behaviors have their own
literatures — name, adapt, or refer per supervision. Ego-syntonic perfectionism without
intrusions (OCPD's architecture) is a different building.

**The accommodation census.** OCD uniquely annexes the household: the spouse answering the
same question nightly, the parent doing the laundry rite, the family's routes and menus bent
around the rituals. Map it at intake — who provides what reassurance, who performs what ritual
by proxy, what the household may not do — because Phase 4 winds it down and the family are
co-therapists or counter-therapists, never bystanders.

**Scrupulosity's ally.** Where the content is religious, the protocol targets the disorder and
never the faith — the machine has taken the faith hostage, and treatment returns it. With
consent, the client's own clergy can be enlisted (most traditions have doctrine for exactly
this; confessors have known scrupulosity for centuries) so that exposures are built inside the
client's actual theology, not against it.

**The medication boundary.** SSRIs at OCD dosing are legitimate, evidence-backed company —
alongside, not instead; nothing here outranks the prescriber.

## Phase 1 — Locate and formulate (the riser profile)

Two sessions of ordinary listening, Appendix A machinery with a gate filter. The sorting
questions:

- *"What does the thought accuse you of?"* The obsessional content, taken down verbatim and
  then read back through the values map: what would have to matter this much for the machine
  to guard it this hard? The client hears their own indictment reframed as an inventory of
  what they love — the protocol's first mismatch, delivered in session one or two.
- *"What must you do when it arrives — and what happens if you can't?"* The full ritual
  inventory, with the covert menu explicitly hunted: the counting, the silent undoing prayer,
  the mental review of the drive home, the confession disguised as conversation, the
  reassurance question worn as curiosity. **Miss the mental rituals and Phase 4 will run
  exposure with the back door open** — the single commonest failure in this riser.
- *"How sure do you have to be before you can stop?"* The certainty demand, quantified. "One
  hundred percent" is the tell, and the number gets written down for Phase 3 to retry.
- *"Whose standards run this courtroom?"* Floor 6 and floor 8: the house where mistakes were
  moral events, the blamed child, the doctrine, the coincidence that convicted (the thought
  that preceded the grandmother's stroke). The commission of the charter, located.

Baseline the furnace in numbers: Y-BOCS, ritual log (episodes, minutes per day), avoidance
map, the accommodation census, reassurance count, insight rating. Minutes per day is the
number families feel first when it falls.

## Phase 2 — Teach the client the machine

Client-facing telling, in the model's images — and in this riser the dignity move is a
*demonstration*, not a speech: the normal-intrusion inventory read together (the published
lists of what ordinary minds produce — the shoving, the swerving, the stove, the sacrilege),
so the client sees their catalog on everyone's shelf. Then: **your machine does not have worse
thoughts — it has a gate that flags them and a courtroom that tries them.** The values-map
claim lands here: the harm thought in a loving parent is the machine checking its most
precious cargo; a person who cared nothing for the child would get no alarm. Then the claims
that reorganize everything, stated flat:

*A thought is not a message.* *The felt certainty you're waiting for does not exist — for
anyone.* And: *the ritual is the engine, not the brake.*

## Phase 3 — Floor 2 first, because floor 2 is the gatekeeper

**The gate re-taught: noise versus message.** The client learns to classify rather than
adjudicate: intrusions are weather from the machine room, and the skill is spotting the
*form* (intrusive, urgent, demanding certainty, threatening fault) rather than debating the
*content*. The engagement rule is the discipline that carries every later phase: the thought
gets **neither obedience nor rebuttal** — no ritual, and no argument, reassurance,
disproof, or mental trial either, because engagement of any polarity files it as mail. Spam,
not mail: noticed, named, left unopened.

**The charter retried.** Salkovskis's machinery in the house style — the responsibility pie
drawn for a feared event (every contributing cause sliced before the client's thought gets
its sliver); the double-standard instrument, written ("your friend confides this exact
thought — what does it prove about him?"); the omission audit (the charter bills omissions
like commissions — no court on earth does). The certainty demand renamed as the impossible
product: every stopped ritual in Phase 4 is also a certainty the client agreed to live
without, and the gate learns the deadband every other mind runs on.

![[ocd-responsibility-pie.png]]

*The pie — an instrument, drawn in session, in the client's handwriting.*

**The demonstration experiment.** Checking-degrades-confidence, run live per van den Hout:
the stove (or lock, or email) checked once versus checked ten times, memory confidence rated
each round — the client watches the tenth check return *less* certainty than the first, in
their own numbers. The remedy indicted by its own output; Phase 4's consent signs itself.

![[ocd-checking-curve.png]]

*The checking curve — the shape after van den Hout & Kindt; the client's own numbers go on the
teal circles.*

Deliverable: consent to ERP, informed and specific. "We're going to let the thought be
weather while your hands stay still — and read what actually happens."

## Phase 4 — Interrupt the furnace (floor 5): exposure with the door held shut

ERP, run in the house format as forecast experiments — six steps (prediction named →
confidence rated → **written before** → smallest real trial → compared out loud → "how many
times before the rule revises?"), with response prevention as the non-negotiable second half:
**exposure without prevention is rehearsal of the loop,** and worse than nothing.

- **The menu is built from the profile:** contact with the contaminated handle, then the
  unwashed sandwich; the door locked once and left; the email sent unreviewed; the knife
  washed beside the spouse; being last out of the building; the child bathed. Each trial's
  forecast is the charter's, verbatim: *"if I touch it and don't wash, the family gets sick
  and it's mine — 85%."*
- **The covert menu is blocked by name:** no counting, no undoing prayer, no mental review
  after, no confession-by-anecdote at dinner, no reassurance question in session — the
  therapist holds the door too.
- **Imaginal exposure carries the unactable content:** the harm scene, the blasphemy, the
  hit-and-run already "committed" — written as a script and held as weather, supervised and
  titrated, because content that can't be staged can still be filed correctly.
- **The reassurance economy winds down on schedule:** the family briefed in the model's
  images (their answer is the ritual's second performer; withdrawal is love wearing work
  clothes), replacement scripts supplied, accommodations retired one per week with the
  client's consent and the family's relief tracked — it arrives.
- **The counterattack, named in advance:** *reinterpret* ("nothing happened because I was
  careful in some new way" — variation and responsibility-loading answer it: the client
  locks up alone, cooks for someone, goes last); *filter* (clean trials uncounted — the
  written ledger holds them); the **ratchet**, this riser's shell game, forecast so it can't
  ambush: content will migrate — contamination falls and morality rises, the machine changes
  costume — and the client learns the sentence *same gate, same answer* before the first
  migration arrives.

Design rules per Craske, as everywhere: expectancy violation over habituation; sized so the
machine shows up; varied, not laddered by comfort; run to the read-out. **Session metric
(§6.2): if nothing surprised the client, nothing changed.**

## Phase 5 — Supply the readings (floor 7, the dimmer, and the repatriated day)

- **The hours come home.** Minutes-per-day is the number that falls first, and the protocol
  spends the recovered hours on purpose: the values the content held hostage get scheduled
  in the ritual's old slots — the loving parent actually on the floor with the child, not
  checking on her; the devout actually praying, not neutralizing. The mattering dial gets
  paid in its own currency at last.
- **Floor 7 serviced.** Sleep restored per the pinned block (an exhausted gate flags
  everything; OCD runs worse on debt), stimulants audited, the body's arousal given legal
  outlets — the standard readings, delivered while the loop is interrupted.
- **The dimmer.** Shame is this riser's oldest cellmate — decades of believing the thoughts
  were confessions, told to no one. The therapy relationship disconfirms it weekly: the worst
  content, spoken aloud, met without a flinch and filed as weather. Where the furnace eats it
  ("she'd recoil if she really understood"), the move is named — and answered by the
  supervisor-backed steadiness of a clinician who has actually heard the content and stayed.
- **Scrupulosity's readings:** ordinary practice restored inside the client's tradition —
  prayer at the doctrine's cadence rather than the gate's, the confessor's actual counsel
  followed rather than re-polled — faith returned from the hostage-taker.

## Phase 6 — The ignition scene, if Phase 1 found one

Where a floor-6 trace commissioned the charter — the child blamed for the accident ("you
should have been watching"), the house where error was sin, the coincidence that convicted
(the angry wish and the grandmother's stroke in the same week), the scrupulous formation —
ERP moves the rituals and the responsibility *feeling* persists, dateless, or the ratchet
runs endless because every jurisdiction sits on the same bedrock verdict. The trace needs
activation-plus-mismatch — the scene revisited while regulated, the eight-year-old's
conviction retried with the adult's evidence (a child is not the family's fire marshal; a
wish is not a weapon), the charter's commission formally ended — within scope and
supervision, or by referral until licensure.

## Phase 7 — Consolidation up the building

Now floor 3 writes, because the ledger exists for the sentences to describe: the client
states the revised model in their own words — the false-alarm count, the certainty audit,
the checked-versus-once numbers — never the therapist's absolution, which the gate would
re-poll by Thursday. Floor 1 re-authors: *the dangerous one, the dirty one, the damned one*
becomes *the one whose gate flagged everything precious, and who taught it to read again* —
made, not found, and said out loud. Where floor-8 water is in play — purity codes, doctrine
weaponized, the certainty-worship of the client's field (medicine, law, engineering all pay
the charter's wages) — it gets named and renegotiated. And the values the content mapped get
their legal channels: care enacted directly, at experiment scale first, then as a life.

## Phase 8 — Relapse as forecast, not failure

The ratchet is this riser's relapse mode: under load the machine returns in new content, and
the model says so in advance — the flare is identified by **form, not content** (urgency +
ritual demand + certainty demand = the machine, whatever the costume). The written flare
protocol: the sentence rehearsed (*same gate, same answer*); one ERP rep inside the week, no
reassurance detour first; the family briefed on early warnings (accommodation requests
returning, the question asked twice, minutes creeping); the spare-jurisdiction audit (the
domain never treated — often mental rituals — checked before it becomes the annex). Booster
sessions are maintenance, not failure. Close by reviewing the numbers — Y-BOCS, minutes per
day, the accommodation census — as the story of the treatment.

## Two therapist-side rules, all phases

1. **Never argue with the verdict — and never notarize it either.** Reassurance is this
   riser's ritual and the therapist is its favorite notary: answer "but are you *sure* it's
   OCD?" twice and the session is the compulsion, co-signed. The same trap wears clinical
   clothes — re-clarifying content, safety-probing the same obsession weekly — therapist
   rituals, both. Warm non-engagement with the content, full engagement with the person, and
   the discipline of holding the door while the reading writes.
2. **Match the tempo, and watch the transfer rule.** ERP outpaced is consent outrun —
   collaborative or nothing, per the header — but under-paced is furnace maintenance;
   supervision holds the line between. And the gate generalizes: expect the machinery in
   health anxiety's jurisdiction, scrupulosity's, perfectionism's — the certainty demand is
   the common thread, and the client who can say *same gate, same answer* owns every future
   costume. Say so before the first migration.
