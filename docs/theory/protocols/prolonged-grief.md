# Protocol — Prolonged Grief, derived from the model

19 August 2026. Filed in Clinical Tools beside the Decision Aid and the other protocols.
**Honesty label, in the appendix spirit: made, not found.** This is a model-derived
reorganization of components that existing grief treatments have tested separately —
complicated grief treatment (Shear; the strongest RCT base in this drawer's
neighborhood), the cognitive-behavioral model of prolonged grief (Boelen), the exposure
and behavioral-activation lineages, the dual-process model (Stroebe & Schut), and the
continuing-bonds literature. The *sequence* is the model's contribution (the time gate →
riser profile → the re-addressing → gatekeeper → the two armors → deliberate encounters →
ignition → the bond revised → waves as forecast), and the sequence has no outcome data of
its own. **The scope line, stated before anything else: this protocol refuses to treat
grief that is simply grief. Mourning is not a disorder, most people complete it with
accompaniment and time, and the time gate below is diagnostic law, not a suggestion. A
student's lane is the gates and the accompaniment; the confrontation work at the center
runs under supervision.** Companions: [[floor-notes/Floor 4 — Relational Templates]],
[[floor-notes/Floor 6 — Procedure and Emotion]], the furnace (§2.3), [[Protocol —
Depression]] (the differential), [[Protocol — PTSD]] (the two-files rule), and the
Craske, Kube, and Joiner pins in the ledger.

## The formulation in one line

Prolonged grief presents as sorrow that will not move, but the engine is floor 4 — a
relational template still issuing predictions for a person the world no longer contains —
kept unrevised by floor 5's twin armors (avoidance of the absence, and ritual proximity
to the presence), policed by floor 2's betrayal gatekeeper (*updating is abandoning*),
narrated by floor 1 as a story that cannot be told to its end, in floor-8 water that
schedules mourning and then punishes both the fast and the slow — and the treatment lets
the template meet the evidence at a survivable dose until the forecast revises, which is
not forgetting; it is filing.

## The model's distinctive reading

Three claims organize everything below.

**Grief is the update; prolonged grief is the update refused.** The template encoded the
person at the deepest layer the machine has — *will be there, will answer, will come home*
— and the encoding ran for years or decades at maximal precision, confirmed daily. Death
deletes the person from the world but not from the predictor. So every reminder is a
collision: the machine renders presence, the world returns absence, and the pang *is* the
prediction error — the love's machinery meeting the fact. Healthy mourning is thousands
of these collisions, each one filing a little of the revision, which is why it hurts in
waves and why it slowly changes. Prolonged grief is the collisions prevented: the error
fires and updates nothing, so year three hurts like week three — gone from the world,
undiminished in the forecast. The machine's cruelest feature here is its most loyal one:
it keeps predicting the person because it learned them so well.

**Both armors serve the same refusal.** The obvious armor is avoidance — the room
unentered, the photographs boxed, the name unspoken at dinner, the grave unvisited — which
starves the update of evidence. Its opposite twin is the shrine — the room unchanged, the
voicemail replayed nightly, the texts still sent, the place still set — which feeds the
template's *still here* forecast directly. The model reads both as one move: protecting
the template from the revision. This is the reading that reorganizes the clinical
picture, because the family usually attacks one armor while the client holds the other,
and both sides are right about the other's armor and wrong about the mechanism — and
because the treatment for both is the same: dosed, deliberate contact with the evidence.

**The gatekeeper's verdict is betrayal, and it is never argued with.** *If I stop
grieving, I abandon them. If the pain fades, the love fades. Healing is forgetting.* The
client is not failing to accept the death; they are refusing to file it, because filing
feels like performing the death themselves — a second killing, done by their own hand.
The protocol's answer is never argument; it is the experiment: test what filing actually
costs the love. The finding — run, never promised — is the treatment's deepest result:
the bond survives the update. Love does not need the wrong forecast. What comes out the
far side is not absence of relationship but a revised one: the person carried, instead
of expected.

## Phase 0 — The gates (the calendar first, and the heaviest risk work in the drawer)

**The time gate, absolute.** Acute grief is not a disorder at any intensity. The
diagnostic lines are stated honestly — DSM-5-TR's prolonged grief disorder requires
twelve months (six for a child's bereavement); ICD-11 draws it at six — and this protocol
does not open before the client's own tradition and calendar have been read, because
floor 8 sets mourning's tempo differently across cultures and the manual's clock is not
the only clock. Before the line: accompaniment, practical help, normalization, and no
protocol — pathologizing normal mourning is the specific harm this gate exists to
prevent. The protocol begins where mourning has demonstrably stalled: the acute picture
unchanged, the life unreclaimed, the criteria met.

**Risk, at this drawer's maximum.** Suicide screening at the depression protocol's full
standard, with one addition specific to this riser: reunion ideation — *I just want to be
with them* — asked about plainly and sorted carefully, from passive longing through
intent, because the spectrum is real and the sorting is not optional. Thwarted
belongingness is this riser's native weather, and the Joiner pin stands directly behind
the assessment. Means safety, escalation thresholds pre-agreed, crisis resources in
writing (in the US, 988).

**The depression differential, named.** Prolonged grief's pain is yearning-shaped and
person-shaped — it points at someone. Depression's is verdict-shaped and self-shaped — it
points at the client. They co-occur freely, and where both are present both get treated,
[[Protocol — Depression]] alongside, sequencing per supervision.

**The two-files rule.** Where the death was violent, sudden, or witnessed, two files
exist: the scene and the absence. The unfiled scene blocks access to the unfiled loss —
you cannot mourn a person while the machine is still mid-emergency about how they died —
so [[Protocol — PTSD]]'s machinery runs first or alongside, and the imagery of the death
is handled under its treaty rules, not this protocol's.

**Circumstance honesty.** Suicide bereavement carries its own weather — the shame, the
anger that feels forbidden, the endless why — and its own supports, named and offered.
The ambivalent or estranged relationship complicates the template with an unfinished
quarrel: grief for a person one also raged at files slowly, and the protocol makes room
for both truths without forcing a verdict. Caregiver relief-guilt — the exhale after a
long dying, prosecuted by the client as a crime — gets the desert move early.

**The medication boundary.** No medication treats grief. Comorbid depression's
pharmacology is the prescriber's lane, routed as always.

## Phase 1 — Locate and formulate (the riser profile)

The five questions, and then the inventory this riser needs:

- **The template's contents.** Not just who died but what the template held: the only
  witness to the client's history, the organizer of every holiday, the one who called
  first, the person whose imagined verdict still runs the client's decisions. The
  treatment must know what offices the dead held, because Phase 7 redistributes them.
- **The death, at headline resolution.** Told with the PTSD protocol's handle rule where
  it runs hot: labeled, not opened, the client holding the depth control from minute one.
- **The two-armor inventory.** What is avoided (rooms, dates, songs, people, the grave)
  and what is enshrined (the untouched closet, the replayed voicemail, the still-active
  phone plan) — collected without a flicker of judgment, because both lists are love's
  handwriting.
- **The betrayal verdicts, verbatim.** *Moving on means he didn't matter. If I laugh,
  I've forgotten her.* These are Phase 3's targets and Phase 6's experiment.
- **The calendar.** Anniversaries, birthdays, the diagnosis date, the date itself —
  mapped in advance, because Phase 8 plans them and ambush is the alternative.
- **The ecology.** Who else is grieving, how the family's armors interlock, and what the
  world did — the casseroles that stopped at month two, the friends who crossed the
  street, the workplace's three days of leave: floor 8's mourning deadline, named as
  water, not as the client's failure.

Baselines: PG-13 or ICG, PHQ-9 with the item-9 follow-up standing, sleep log, and the
life-shrinkage map — what the loss has annexed, drawn honestly.

## Phase 2 — Teach the client the machine

Client-facing telling, in the model's images, and the dignity move leads: **the pang is
not a malfunction and not a verdict that you are stuck — it is the love's machinery
meeting the fact, which is the update trying to happen.** The predictor telling: *for
thirty years your machine learned him — his tread on the stairs, his side of the bed, the
pause before he answered — and it learned him so well that it still renders him. Death
removed him from the world; nothing has yet removed him from the forecast. That collision
you feel at the supermarket is the machine doing the only thing it knows: predicting the
person it loved, and meeting the aisle where he isn't.*

Then the claims that reorganize everything, stated flat: *grief is the update arriving in
waves — and the waves are survivable, and they crest. The pain is not the enemy of the
love; the pain is the love, doing accounting.*

## Phase 3 — Retire the gatekeeper's rules (floor 2)

1. **The betrayal gatekeeper, named — never debated.** *Updating is abandoning* is the
   riser's load-bearing belief, and it is treated as Phase 6's experiment, not tonight's
   argument. For now it is surfaced, written verbatim, and honored as what it is: loyalty,
   miswired.
2. **The counterfactual circling.** *If I'd made him go to the doctor — if I'd called
   that morning — why didn't I see it* — the orbit that feels like duty and functions as
   avoidance, circling the loss instead of entering it. The depression protocol's
   two-minute test transfers whole: has any lap of this orbit ever produced anything but
   the same sentence at higher volume? The guilt gets sorted honestly: the rare accurate
   remainder is handled with the moral-injury logic — accurate sizing, amends where any
   exist, meaning — and the rest gets the desert move: the loss was real; *deserved* was
   the forged signature.
3. **The wave protocol, taught early.** For the ambushes — the aisle, the song, the
   handwriting on an old list — the deadband work: the wave crests; ride it where it
   found you, or postpone the full visit to a chosen hour, but do not build a life
   around never being found.

## Phase 4 — Retire the two armors (floor 5, the furnace)

Both armors retire by negotiated experiment, never by decree, in the house six-step
format (prediction named → confidence rated → **written before** → smallest real trial →
compared out loud → "how many times before the rule revises?"):

- **The avoidance armor:** the room entered, the box opened at dose, the name said at
  dinner, the photograph returned to the shelf, the grave visited — smallest survivable
  first, ladder upward.
- **The shrine armor, revised at dose:** the voicemail kept but not replayed nightly;
  the closet sorted one shelf at a time, with chosen keepings; the place at the table
  sometimes filled. Revision, not demolition — the protocol distinguishes ritual that
  serves the bond from ritual that serves the forecast, and the client makes the call,
  item by item.
- **The oscillation legitimized.** The healthy rhythm alternates — hours in the loss,
  hours in the life — and neither is a betrayal of the other (the dual-process finding,
  pinned below). The good hour gets counted on purpose, because the furnace's *filter*
  discards it as disloyalty.
- **The counterattack, named in advance.** *Filter* (the laugh uncounted, the good day
  reclassified as forgetting); *reinterpret* ("I only coped because I stayed busy");
  *provoke* (the fight on the anniversary — the furnace manufacturing familiar weather);
  *withdraw effort* (the invitations declined until they stop coming — the world's
  evidence of continued life, evacuated); *prepayment* (the future losses pre-grieved,
  the new closeness refused because it too will end — named when it appears, because it
  is the template generalizing its wound).

## Phase 5 — Supply the readings (the deliberate encounter)

The house reading, in this riser: **the collision run on purpose**, at chosen dose, with
the surprise metric out loud. Forecast written before: *I will not survive the photo
album.* Reading compared after: *I wept for ten minutes; I showed my daughter the fishing
picture; I laughed once; the wave crested and I was still at the table.* Craske's rules
as everywhere — varied, repeated, run to the readout.

And the telling as filing: the death's story told start to finish, aloud, repeatedly —
Shear's revisiting, read by the model as the same engine as everywhere in the drawer,
activation-plus-mismatch: the loss contacted while the present's evidence (a steady
witness, a living room, a life still running) is in the room, until the story acquires a
date and can be told without the siren. The dimmer carries its usual load: grief spoken in
accurate company, ladder-first — the safest person, not the most important — because the
floor-8 deadline taught the client that their grief is too much for people, and that
forecast needs data too.

## Phase 6 — The ignition scene (the heaviest door, and the true sentence)

The centerpiece: the heaviest avoided thing, met at full formality — the grave unvisited
for three years, the closet, the restaurant, the song played through, the ashes finally
addressed — planned like an expedition, dosed to be survivable and real, with the true
sentence said out loud in the place: ***gone.*** Said while the love is also in the room,
which is the entire design — the template meeting the fact at maximum signal, with the
witness steady and the window held. Where the unsaid is the weight, the imaginal
conversation runs here (Shear's tool, under supervision): the goodbye the death never
allowed, the apology, the answer imagined honestly — not séance, rehearsal: floor 6
completing a procedure it was never allowed to finish. **The tell that the filing took:**
the memory becomes visitable at will — sorrow without siren, the PTSD protocol's tell,
transferred whole because the machinery rhymes: filed is not forgotten; filed is when it
gets a date on it and stops being tonight.

## Phase 7 — Consolidation: the bond, revised

The template is revised, never deleted — and the protocol says so in exactly those
words, because this phase is what the betrayal gatekeeper predicted could not exist.
Continuing bonds, on updated terms: the internal relationship rebuilt as counsel rather
than expectation — *what would she say* carried deliberately, the birthday toast kept on
purpose, the nightly voicemail retired by choice. The offices redistributed honestly:
who organizes the holidays now, who gets called first, who witnesses — some offices go
to the living, and some the client learns to hold alone, and the grief for each
redistribution is legitimate. Floor 1 re-authors: the story told to its end *and past
it* — widow, widower, the one who lost — permitted as a chapter, protected from
annexing the book. Floor 8 honored: the tradition's mourning forms used where they
serve (the yahrzeit, the ofrenda, the anniversary mass — the cultures that
institutionalized deliberate encounters solved half this protocol centuries ago). And
the life re-entered on the calendar, because the person cannot come back, and the
client can.

## Phase 8 — Waves as forecast, not failure

Waves forever, by design — and the model says so in advance: **grief is not a task that
completes; it is a tide that changes.** The honest curve from Phase 2 is re-drawn at
termination with the client's own data on it: down, with wobble, never to zero, and zero
was never the goal. The calendar pre-planned: anniversaries met deliberately — the
protocol's rule of thumb: *meet the date before it meets you.* The flare card: the wave
protocol, one deliberate encounter within the week (evacuated ground re-annexes), the
safe person's number, and the reminder in the client's own hand that a hard month is the
tide, not the treatment failing. New losses will re-open this file and old ones — named
in advance as the machinery working, not the work undone. Boosters at the hard dates.
Close on the baselines — the PG-13 arc, the map re-expanded, the offices redistributed —
as the story of the treatment.

## Two therapist-side rules, all phases

1. **Never argue with the verdict — and never compete with the dead or hurry the love.**
   The betrayal belief is tested by experiment, never debated at the whiteboard. The
   dead are spoken of with the client's own reverence, by name, in the tense the client
   chooses. And the therapist's face is data at maximal precision: any flicker of
   discomfort at tears reads as *your grief is too much* — the exact floor-8 sentence
   that built the armor — so composure here is not neutrality; it is the disconfirming
   evidence, delivered weekly.
2. **Match the tempo, and watch the transfer rule.** Mourning has no deadline and the
   protocol has phases anyway; the tension is managed out loud, with the client holding
   the calendar. Expect the machinery in adjacent costumes: the pre-grieving of the
   living, the goodbye-avoidance at every airport, the new closeness kept at arm's
   length because it too will end. And expect the update to open old files — the mother
   lost at twelve arrives when the husband is finally mourned, because the machine
   files losses in the same drawer — the transfer rule at its gentlest and deepest, and
   it gets room, not a referral reflex.
