# Protocol — The Self-Story (Low Self-Esteem), derived from the model

19 August 2026. Filed in Clinical Tools beside the Decision Aid and the other protocols.
**This is the drawer's transdiagnostic protocol: it treats a process, not a diagnosis.** It
runs two ways — standalone, where the presenting complaint is the verdict itself (chronic
worthlessness, the inner prosecutor, a life priced down) without a full syndrome; and as a
**module inside any other protocol** when its process is found load-bearing (the depression
that lifts and leaves the verdict standing; the social anxiety whose feared exhibit is the
defective self; the perfectionism running every riser's armor). The model's whole argument
is that it treats mechanisms rather than labels — this protocol is that argument, on paper.
**Honesty label, in the appendix spirit: made, not found.** A model-derived reorganization
of components tested separately — Fennell's low self-esteem model, Swann's self-verification
program (pinned, twice), the expectation-persistence work (Kube, pinned), compassion-focused
therapy's stance. The *sequence* is the model's contribution and has no outcome data of its
own. Written by a counseling student; runs under supervision; composes with, never replaces,
program training and site protocols. Companions: [[floor-notes/Floor 1 — Narrative]],
[[floor-notes/Floor 4 — Relational Templates]], [[floor-notes/Floor 2 — Metacognition]], the
furnace (§2.3), and the Swann, Kube, and Joiner pins in the ledger.

## The formulation in one line

The self-story presents as a floor-1 verdict (*not enough — defective, unlovable, less-than*)
enforcing floor-3 sentences daily, defended by floor-5 treaties (the rules for living:
perform, please, hide), prosecuted by a floor-2 officer (the inner critic as policy), cast
through floor-4 templates (a supporting cast selected to confirm it), usually written on
floor 6 (the scenes where a child concluded correctly from terrible data), soaked in floor-8
water (meritocracy, comparison feeds, the family's worth-currency), and taxing both dials at
once — and the treatment follows the building, from the oldest floor down.

## The model's distinctive reading

Three claims organize everything below.

**The self-story is a forecast engine, not a fact archive.** *I am not enough* presents as a
description and operates as the building's master prior: it predicts (rejection, failure,
exposure), prices (what may be asked for, what treatment is deserved, which rooms are for
people like this), and pre-pays (the applications not sent, the person not approached, the
sentence at the meeting not said). Fennell's bottom line, in the model's terms: the oldest
verdict in the building — written early, by a child, from a child's data — running the whole
house from a floor nobody visits. This is §2.1 at the identity level: the client is not
perceiving a lesser self; they are perceiving the machine's render of one, updated nightly.

**The machine prefers coherence to comfort — the story defends itself.** The pinned Swann
program is this protocol's spine: people seek and keep feedback, partners, and situations
that *confirm* their self-view — even a negative one — because a predicted world outranks a
kind one; confirmation files as safety, disconfirmation as error. So the praise bounces and
the criticism sticks (Kube's immunization running at the identity level: the compliment
re-explained before it lands — *politeness, pity, they don't really know me*), the cast gets
selected for agreement (the partner who confirms you're lucky to have them; the friends who
need you small), and the furnace runs the full set: *filter* (the wins deleted nightly),
*reinterpret* (the promotion explained away), *provoke* (the fishing for criticism, the
self-deprecation that begs correction and files the correction as pity — Joiner's pin), and
*withdraw effort* at the identity level (*not for people like me* — the rooms never entered,
compiling the undefeated record).

**The treaties produce the competence and confiscate the credit.** The rules for living —
Fennell's machinery, the model's floor-5 treaties — are the deals a child signed with the
verdict: *IF I am perfect / always pleasing / never needing / invisible, THEN the defect
stays covered.* They work, which is the trap: they build real competence, real likability,
real reliability — and every success is credited to the *rule*, never the self (*they only
value me because I overdeliver; drop the mask and watch*). The armor accounting from every
riser in this drawer, run on a whole life: decades of evidence, all filed under the
disguise. The dignity move is structural: the story was **made, not found** — a rational
archivist concluding correctly from the sources available (the contempt, the comparisons,
the chaos, the absence) — and the error isn't the child's arithmetic; it's that the archive
stopped accepting submissions. Treatment is re-opening the file: not affirmations (argument,
and the machine doesn't attend hearings), but engineered anomalies the archive has to log.

## Phase 0 — The gates (run first, and never fully retired)

**Routing and mood.** Where a full depressive syndrome is present, [[Protocol — Depression]]
leads (its gates are heavier) and this protocol runs as its module; risk screens at that
standard regardless, because chronic worthlessness is a load-bearing wall of suicidality —
the mattering dial's readings are taken seriously here, every time.

**The accurate-history gate.** Some archives contain atrocities. Where the verdict was
installed by abuse, the scenes belong to [[Protocol — PTSD]]'s machinery and this protocol
composes with it rather than running experiments over an unfiled wound. The sorting
question: is the archive's problem *bad inference from bad treatment* (this protocol) or
*unmetabolized scenes still timestamped NOW* (that one first, or both, sequenced by
supervision)?

**The pattern-scope note.** Where the self-story runs with pervasive instability — identity
diffusion, self-harm, frantic relational weather — the picture may be a personality-level
architecture with its own standard of care (DBT-informed work, longer arcs, specialist
referral). This protocol serves; it does not substitute.

**The attention differential.** One presentation in this drawer's orbit routinely arrives
with an ADHD diagnosis attached: the serial starter — focus deep and intact inside any
chosen project, but a history of abandoned vehicles, each launched on a fresh forecast of
recognition (*this one gets me seen*) and dropped when that forecast dims. That kinetics
can be valuation, not attention: the rank machinery steering, with the mattering dial
wired through it. Sort it honestly, in both directions. Hyperfocus never rules ADHD out —
interest-driven attention is a classic feature of the real condition — and the two
co-occur freely: a childhood of ADHD verdicts (*careless, behind, wasted potential*) is
exactly the soil this protocol's archive grows in, so the diagnosis can be right and the
pattern still running on top of it. The discriminating questions: were the low-stakes
mechanics broken in childhood, before any recognition motive existed (lost items,
evaporated homework, drift mid-conversation)? Does necessary tedium hold when the prize is
still believed? And what does the mind wander *to* — anything at all (attention), or the
next audience (the story)? Formal assessment is the assessor's lane; this protocol's lane
is the forecast the projects serve — and where the pattern is the story's, the recognition
forecast gets named, written down, and tested like every other verdict in this drawer.
(Stimulant response settles nothing either way; stimulants sharpen everyone.)

**The cultural calibration.** Floor 8 before floor 1: in some waters self-effacement is
ordinary dignity, humility is currency, and the "confident" target this protocol's
literature assumes is itself a cultural artifact. Norm to the client's culture (the
one-page's discipline): treat the *suffering* — the prosecutor, the priced-down life — never
the culture's manners.

## Phase 1 — Locate and formulate (the riser profile)

Two sessions of ordinary listening, Appendix A machinery with an identity filter. The
sorting questions:

- *"When is the earliest you remember already believing it?"* The verdict's birthday, and
  the floor-6 scene-map around it: the report card at the kitchen table, the comparison
  sibling, the parent's face, the schoolyard. Most clients can date the verdict to single
  digits — which is itself the first mismatch, said gently: *who did the research?*
- *"What are the rules — what must you always do, and never do, so it doesn't show?"* The
  treaties, itemized and written: the perfectionism, the pleasing, the invisibility, the
  self-sufficiency ("never need"), the pre-emptive joke. Phase 4 needs the list.
- *"What does the voice say when you win? When you rest? When someone loves you?"* The
  prosecutor's filings caught verbatim — the filter and reinterpret moves in the client's
  own vocabulary, which become Phase 3's exhibits.
- *"Who confirms it — and who would be confused if you changed?"* The Swann map, floor 4:
  the cast audited for verification contracts — the partner, the boss, the friend group,
  the family seat — because Phase 4's experiments will wobble some of these, and the
  profile should know which in advance.

Baseline in numbers: Rosenberg Self-Esteem Scale, a one-week prosecutor count (self-critical
hits per day, sampled), the rules list, the asking-count (requests made per week — this
number is usually near zero and its rise is an outcome), pleasure/mastery sampling.

## Phase 2 — Teach the client the machine

Client-facing telling, in the model's images, and the dignity move *is* the telling: **the
story was made, not found — and it was made by a good archivist working from terrible
sources.** A child in that house, with that data, drawing that conclusion, was doing honest
science; the failure isn't in the inference — it's that the file closed early and the
archive stopped logging anomalies, decades ago. Then the claims that reorganize everything,
stated flat:

*The story is a forecast, not a fact.* *The evidence has been curated, not collected.* And:
*your competence is real, and the story has been billing it to the disguise.*

Name the prepayment: a life priced by the verdict — the asks never made, the rooms never
entered, the love half-received through the armor — is prepayment at the largest scale this
drawer treats, and the ledger of it (started gently in Phase 1's asking-count) is treatment
material, not an indictment.

## Phase 3 — Floor 2 first, because floor 2 is the prosecutor

1. **The critic's job description.** The inner prosecutor is a *policy*, not a truth-teller,
   and the policy has a function the client can name: pre-empt others' contempt by getting
   there first; whip the performance that keeps the treaty; enforce the humility that once
   kept a child safe at a table. Honor the function — it was protection — then audit the
   contract: is the whip producing what it promises?
2. **Cost accounting — the motivation audit.** The charter says self-criticism maintains
   standards; the record says the client's best work happens despite the prosecutor, not
   because of it, and the whip's actual yields are procrastination, hiding, and the
   flinch. Run it as data: a week of output under the ordinary regime, read honestly;
   then Phase 5's compassion trial as the two-condition comparison.
3. **The double-standard instrument, in writing.** The friend test run formally: the
   client's own situation, transposed onto a named friend, verdict written — then the two
   verdicts laid side by side. The gap *is* the finding: the client owns a working
   standard of fairness and exempts exactly one person from it.
4. **The anomaly log.** Fennell's positive-data log with the model's rationale stated
   plainly: this is not affirmation, it is *counting* — evidence the filter would delete,
   written down before the nightly delete cycle runs. Three entries a day, however small,
   logged like lab data. The prosecutor will call it cheating; the response is the flag
   from Phase 2 — we're re-opening the archive, and archives log.

## Phase 4 — Interrupt the furnace (floor 5): the treaties tested

The rules for living, broken at experiment scale, six-step format (prediction named →
confidence rated → **written before** → smallest real test → compared out loud → "how many
times before the rule revises?"):

- The perfectionist ships the B+ draft, on schedule, unpolished — forecast: *exposure,
  contempt, the end of the reputation* — and reads what actually returns.
- The pleaser says the small no: *"I can't this week"* — forecast: *the warmth withdraws* —
  and counts what actually withdraws (the depression protocol's fawn experiment, run here
  for its identity payload).
- The self-sufficient one asks — for help, for a preference, for the window seat —
  forecast: *burden, resentment, the debt called in.*
- The invisible one takes space: the comment at the meeting, the disagreement voiced, the
  compliment answered with only *"thank you"* — no deflection, no repayment installment.
- The joker files a sincere sentence before the punchline.

The design rules as everywhere (Craske, pinned): sized so the machine shows up, varied
across rooms and rules, run to the read-out. The finding, collected personally: **the sky
holds.** The warmth mostly doesn't withdraw — and where it visibly *increases*, the reading
is written twice, because the armor was manufacturing the very distance it blamed on the
defect. The counterattack named in advance: *reinterpret* ("they were being polite"),
*filter* (the log holds it), *provoke* caught live (the fishing, the audience-selection —
the Swann move made visible: *notice whose verdict you polled*), and the cast wobble
handled honestly: some relationships are invested in the old contract, and a partner who
punishes the B+ draft or the small no is a *finding* — handled with care, supervision, and
sometimes couples work, never with a unilateral demolition mid-protocol.

**Session metric (§6.2): if nothing surprised the client, nothing changed.**

## Phase 5 — Supply the readings (the dimmer, the body, the water)

- **The dimmer is the master experiment.** The story's deepest clause is *if they really knew
  me, they'd leave* — so being *accurately seen without contempt* is the disconfirmation
  nothing else can substitute for. The therapy relationship runs it weekly (and the furnace
  will eat it on schedule: "paid to care" gets named and used, as everywhere). Then the
  ladder outward, one rung at a time per the one-page's rule — safest person, not the most
  important: one true thing shown, forecast, read. Shame dies in accurate company; the
  protocol engineers the company.
- **The body's vote.** The prosecutor is somatic — the flinch, the collapsed posture, the
  held breath — and the compassion stance runs as a *two-condition experiment*, not a
  vibe: two weeks of deliberate self-compassion practice (the tone the client would use
  with the named friend, applied on schedule) against the forecast *"I'll go soft; the
  standards will collapse"* — output and mood both read. The strong finding in the
  literature and usually in the ledger: standards survive; the flinch doesn't.
- **The water metered.** Floor 8 runs an IV drip into this riser: the comparison feeds,
  the metric dashboards, the family gatherings priced in achievements. The feed audit runs
  like the caffeine audit — dose counted, two-week reduction trial, mood read — and the
  family table gets named for what it is: the water the story was written in, revisited
  annually.

## Phase 6 — The ignition scenes, if Phase 1 found them (it usually did)

This riser nearly always has scenes — the verdict has a birthday, and often an address.
Where experiments move behavior but the verdict re-asserts dateless (*"I know it's not
true, but it's true"* — the exact tell of a floor-6 prior under a floor-3 insight), the
scenes need activation-plus-mismatch: revisited while regulated, the child's conclusion
honored as the best available science of an eight-year-old — and formally retired on the
adult's evidence; the report card re-read by someone who now knows what a child is; the
comparison re-tried with the full record in. Within scope and supervision, or by referral
until licensure; where the scenes are abuse, [[Protocol — PTSD]] leads. The update this
phase writes is the one Phase 7 consolidates: the sources were wrong, and the archivist
was never the defect.

## Phase 7 — Consolidation up the building

Now floor 1 writes — and in this protocol that is the home floor, so the re-authoring is
the centerpiece, not the epilogue: the old bottom line and the new one both written, in
the client's words, from the client's ledger. The new one is deliberately modest and
evidenced — not *I am amazing* (a forecast the machine would rightly refuse) but *I am
ordinary in the way everyone is ordinary: worth not on trial today* — made, not found,
and said out loud. The treaties renegotiate into preferences: excellence *chosen*, kindness
*chosen* — same behaviors, opposite contract, because chosen excellence can ship a B+ on a
tired week without a constitutional crisis. The cast gets its honest accounting: which
relationships renegotiated, which are outgrown, which seats at which tables get taken
differently now. The desert move runs at full depth — the readings were real; *deserved*
was forged. And both dials get their legal channels: mattering fed by contribution and
contact done visibly, as themselves; rank fed by real competence owned in daylight, with
the reference class swapped from the feed to the field.

## Phase 8 — Relapse as forecast, not failure

The old story re-asserts under load and *in the old rooms* — and the model says so in
advance: the file re-opens in the building where it was written, so the parents' kitchen
at Thanksgiving, the reunion, the performance review will each re-run the render, on
schedule. The written flare protocol: the sentence rehearsed (*that's the old forecast;
the ledger says otherwise*); the anomaly log resumed same day; one deliberate rule-break
inside the week; the asking-count checked. Early-warning signature named while well: the
apology creep, the deflected compliments, the asking-count falling, the feed hours
rising, the B+ drafts polished at midnight again. Booster sessions are maintenance, not
failure. Close by reviewing the numbers — the Rosenberg arc, the prosecutor count, the
asking-count — as the story of the treatment.

## Two therapist-side rules, all phases

1. **Never argue with the verdict — and be careful with praise.** Affirmation is this
   riser's classic malpractice: the compliment gets immunized on contact, and — Swann's
   own data — disconfirmation delivered too warm, too fast, too soon reads as *unsafe*
   (the therapist who likes me is a therapist who doesn't know me), and the client who
   feels misread leaves. Titrate warmth to tolerance; let the ledger do the praising; be
   the one audience whose accuracy the client cannot impeach — seen precisely *and* kept
   anyway is the dimmer's whole cargo.
2. **Match the tempo, and watch the transfer rule — and don't prescribe your own cure.**
   The one-page's third discipline lives in this protocol: if striving saved *you*, the
   temptation is to hand this client the climb — but the climb is usually their treaty
   already, running at full tension. The process outlives syndromes: the depression
   lifts, the anxiety quiets, and the verdict waits — which is why this module runs
   wherever the drawer finds it load-bearing, and why its costume changes (perfectionism
   at work after the pleasing retires at home) are forecast before they arrive.
