# Case Formulation — One Page

*A working frame from [[A Unified Clinical Model of Psychotherapy]]. Answer the five questions **with** the client — out loud, co-owned — and assemble the response. Not a protocol — a lens to hold alongside your training and your risk judgment.*

> [!danger] Before anything else
> **Acute risk governs.** Active suicidality, danger to others, abuse, crisis → your standard risk-assessment and safety protocols, full stop. This frame helps you *understand* a presentation; it never replaces assessment, safety planning, or the duty to escalate. **Screen for the reversed dial:** if the picture is psychosis or mania (the world over-saturated with meaning, not a prior held too tightly), the "loosen the prior / run experiments" instinct is wrong and can destabilize — refer, consider medication, use that standard of care instead.

---

## The five questions

**1 · WHERE does the stuck prediction live?**  *(the floor)*
Which level is the problem actually on? Find it by the signs, not the story:
- accurate insight that changes nothing → it's *below* where the words are reaching
- the body answers first (heat, constriction, numbness, drop) → floors 6–7
- the same pattern every time, across partners/jobs/decades → floor 4, relational template
- it shows up *with you* (managing, testing, going compliant/opaque) → floor 4, in the room
- the reaction too big for the occasion → follow the flare to the high-precision prior
- the client's verbs: "I believe / I tell myself" (high) · "I always end up" (scripts) · "I just shut down / my chest closes" (low)

> Floors, top→bottom: 1 Narrative · 2 Metacognition · 3 Beliefs · 4 Relational template · 5 Scripts/behavior · 6 Procedure/emotion · 7 Body · 8 Cultural substrate. **Glass up top (words reach it); concrete down low (words don't).**

**2 · By what LEVER does it change?**  *(precision)*
Assume the corrective information has already been heard and weighted at zero. The job is not to supply a new belief — it's to **re-weight**: lower confidence on the locked prior, raise it on the disconfirming evidence, so an error that's been arriving all along can finally count.

**3 · Against what OBSTACLE?**  *(the furnace)*
Which move is manufacturing the confirming evidence? **Provoke · Avoid · Filter · Reinterpret · Withdraw effort.** A corrective experience won't land while the client's own behavior is producing the opposite faster than you can. Name the move; plan how to interrupt it long enough for one *unmanufactured* error.

**4 · Under what CONDITIONS?**  *(the relationship)*
Safety and trust are what turn the dimmer up. Without them the signal stays weighted to nothing and no technique reaches its floor. The alliance isn't the backdrop — it's the mechanism. Sequence it *first*, then aim floor-matched technique through the space it opens.

**5 · With what at STAKE?**  *(mattering vs. rank)*
Which social gauge is injured?
- **Mattering** (sociometer — wanted, needed, included) — *local, concrete, reachable from your office.* Joiner's burdensomeness & belonging both live here. Usually the **lethal** one.
- **Rank** (hierometer — standing, position, comparison) — you can't hand it over in 50 minutes; don't pretend it doesn't matter. Recalibrate the *reference class* instead of the rank.

## The intervention engine: prediction-error experiment

**Step 0 — is the prior false?** Some predictions are accurate reads of a punishing environment (contemptuous partner, punishing workplace, real discrimination). If the world will confirm the fear, fix the field first — safety, boundaries, advocacy — and don't run disclosure into it. Then target-select: **ladder first** — safest person, not the most important.

Reframe the risky act as an **experiment**, never a demand to "be vulnerable" (heard as a demand to capitulate). *"I bet you're right — let's get the data."*

1. **Name the prediction** — *"if I tell someone I'm struggling, they'll think less of me."*
2. **Rate confidence + write it down, before** — the number and the writing make the gap undeniable; memory rewrites an unrecorded prediction to fit the result.
3. **Run the smallest real test** — one named person, one true thing, once.
4. **Compare, out loud** — the gap between predicted and actual *is* the update. Protect this step; a rushed session drops it.
5. **Ask how many times it'd take to revise the rule** — turns one result into a standing experiment.

**Starter kit** (aim each at its floor): *disclosure* — one true thing · the ask · escalation ladder · delayed report · text (relational) · *irreplaceability inventory · unglamorous favor · reference-class swap* (mattering/rank) · *block the manufactured evidence* (furnace) · *build-with-them · dead-air · satiation check* (appetitive wall) · *exposure as expectancy-violation* (fear).

> **Which wall?** Before intervening, log the antecedent: escape fires *after* aversive moments → **defensive**, needs disconfirmation (epistemic). Escape fires out of *neutral/good* states (an idea landed) → **appetitive**, needs capture-the-impulse + a 24-hr delay (behavioral) — there's no false belief to disprove. And for a builder, don't let the experiment program *become* the next build — index card, not a dashboard.

## The three disciplines

- **Desert move:** separate the *reading* from the *verdict*. The rank/loss may be real; "therefore I'm contemptible and deserve it" is the false part. Strip the desert → same fact, different affect (forward agency stays — the steering wheel survives the verdict).
- **Norm to culture:** restraint ≠ pathology in traditions where it's ordinary dignity; choose tests — and markers of "closeness" — the client's world actually uses.
- **Don't prescribe your own cure.** If striving saved *you*, you'll be tempted to hand a stuck client the climb. He tried it; the world didn't cooperate; that's why he's angry. Test your prior before you prescribe it.
